# **To Please the Guru**
## *Straight Talk on Surrender, Purity, and the Sweet Burn of Devotion*

---

### 📕 **THE BACK COVER BLURB**

**This is not a book to be read; it is a fire to be entered.**

In a world that worships academic degrees, prestigious positions, and comfortable compromises, *To Please the Guru* delivers a sharp, unalloyed call to spiritual reality. Culled from the spontaneous, spoken discourses of His Holiness Bhakti Dhīra Dāmodara Swami, this book is a direct handbook for those who are tired of playing the part of a "cosmetic devotee" and are ready to put their heads on the ground in uncheating surrender (*Nirasta-Kuhakam*).

Maharaj does not speak to keep his audience comfortable. With the raw honesty and compassionate urgency of a true spiritual father, he cuts through the dry intellectualism of the mind (*jñāna*) to reveal the purifying, sweet power of active devotion (*bhakti*). Drawing from the heroic missionary examples of his own beloved spiritual master, His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami, and our supreme preceptor, His Divine Grace A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupāda, Maharaj reveals the ultimate secret of spiritual success: **the unhesitating execution of the Guru’s instructions as one’s very life and soul.**

From the raw weeping of Queen Vidarbha in the forest of separation to the profound test of the "empty classroom," from the uncalculating risk of Śrīmatī Lakṣmīdevī to the urgent mandate to "boil the milk" by protecting our children through devotee-centered gurukulas, this book leads the reader out of the dream of material existence and plants them firmly at the lotus feet of Lord Janārdana.

---

### 📖 **THE LEFT JACKET FLAP: THE INTERNAL JOURNEY**

We walk around in human bodies, but *Srimad-Bhagavatam* suggests that human life does not begin with physical birth—it begins with human behavior, and human behavior begins the moment we decide to bow our heads to the ground. 

**Part I: Putting Our Head on the Ground (The Foundation of Devotion)** establishes the uncompromising formula of *bhakti*. Through the legendary sacrifice of Śrīmatī Lakṣmīdevī, who risked her very chastity to feed her spiritual master, Śrīpāda Rāmānujācārya, and his hungry disciples, the reader is challenged to examine their own surrender. Maharaj exposes the disease of spiritual laziness and provides the ultimate shield of protection: a planned day centered around early-morning *sādhana* and loud, distinct chanting of the Holy Name during *Brahma-muhūrta*.

**Part II: Deepening Our Connection (The Internal Battle)** takes the practitioner deep into the science of *aural reception* (hearing through the ears) as the only cure for the cataract of material illusion. Here, the reader learns what it means to drink the boiling-hot sugarcane juice of pure love—a nectar so sweet that even as it burns our material lips, we refuse to stop drinking. As the physical presence of the Guru fades, Maharaj guides the reader through the agonizing grief of separation, dismantling the "orphan mentality" and revealing how a bona fide disciple stands strong in the "empty classroom" of life by taking the Guru's eternal instructions (*vāṇī*) on their head.

---

### 🛠️ **THE RIGHT JACKET FLAP: THE CALL TO ARMS**

Spiritual life is not a passive, safe haven; it is a battlefield. 

**Part III: Crushing Pride and Making Our Sacrifice (The Offering)** transitions from internal purification to active, risk-taking offering. Maharaj delivers a devastating critique of transactional, corporate giving, warning against the "pasha" mode of charity that treats the Deities as business partners. Instead, he elevates the reader to the "apple of Kṛṣṇa’s eye"—the selfless devotee whose heart bleeds for the suffering of conditioned souls and whose only meditation is to relieve the spiritual master of his anxiety.

The book reaches its climax in a historic, visionary call for the future of the Hare Kṛṣṇa movement. Maharaj challenges the spiritual selfishness of constructing grand, empty buildings while failing to prioritize our children. He demands that we "boil the milk" of our devotion by building self-sufficient, devotee-centered educational systems (*Vidyashram*), farming communities, and cow protection programs. To save the next generation from the secular slaughterhouses of consciousness, we must be ready to make the ultimate sacrifice.

### 👤 **AUTHOR BIOGRAPHY: THE SPEAKER ON THE RUG**

**His Holiness Bhakti Dhīra Dāmodara Swami** is a sannyāsi and a widely traveled spiritual leader within the International Society for Krishna Consciousness (ISKCON). Born with a deep, natural spiritual inclination, he adopted full-time monastic life after coming into contact with the sublime teachings of Śrīla Prabhupāda. 

Maharaj was fortunate to spend many years as a close personal servant to his beloved spiritual master, **His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami (Kṛṣṇapāda)**, from whom he received sannyāsa and his sannyāsa recommendation. Maharaj served as an active instrument in Bhakti Tīrtha Swami's pioneering, high-potency missions across West Africa—including Nigeria, Ghana, the Ivory Coast, Liberia, and Zimbabwe—assisting in the establishment of self-sufficient farming communities, clinics, schools, and cottage industries. 

Known for his uncompromising, straightforward, and intensely compassionate style of preaching, Maharaj sits directly on the rug, looking his listeners in the eye, and addressing the deep struggles of their hearts. His life is entirely dedicated to protecting the disciplic succession, pushing devotees to intensify their daily morning *sādhana*, and building a practical, loving future for the next generation of Vaiṣṇavas.

---

### 🛡️ **IN MEMORIAM: THE CARING FATHER**

This book is dedicated to the glorious, eternal legacy of **His Holiness Bhakti Tīrtha Swami (Kṛṣṇapāda)**, a spiritual warrior whose life defied all material odds. From preaching behind the Iron Curtain under Yugoslavian imprisonment and state police surveillance, to building self-sufficient communities in West Africa amidst extreme physical austerities, Gurudev modeled what it means to be a "transparent postman" of the disciplic line. 

His mystical ability to read the hearts of his disciples, his strict prohibition of corporate transactions in favor of absolute devotee care and spiritual security, and his final, sweet phone-call blessing (*“Don't come, stay there... people will see me in you”*) remain the beating heart of this book.
