# "To Please the Guru" — Scorecard
Synthesized from the full 21-chapter audit + front/back matter review + subsequent re-verification passes (Ch.2, Ch.4, Ch.15 corrected after re-audit). Scores are out of 10, judged against the book's own stated standard (faithful spoken voice, non-corporate Heart Check, source-verified pastimes) — not against a generic editing rubric.

**Legend:** 🟢 8.5–10 strong · 🟡 6.5–8.4 solid with real notes · 🔴 <6.5 needs work

**Note on volatility:** chapter files are under active re-editing (most have jumped multiple versions during this review). Scores reflect the last version actually read for each chapter — re-verify before treating any 🔴/🟡 as final.

**Update after re-verification pass:** 13 chapters (1, 3, 5, 6, 9, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21) showed version-number changes and were re-checked item-by-item against the specific issues previously flagged. Real fixes landed in only a few places — **Ch.12** (main stray-editorial-sentence issue removed), **Ch.19** (2 of 4 items fixed), **Ch.3** (partially — 2 of 6 items fixed), and **Ch.1** (the postman-metaphor concern turned out to be a non-issue on closer look, not an actual fix). Everything else re-checked at that point (Ch.5, 6, 9, 13, 16, 17, 18, 21) came back essentially unchanged on the specific items flagged.

**Second update (Ch.6, 9, 14, 15 re-verified again after a further edit pass):** **Chapter 14's Heart Check checkbox violation is now FIXED** — both checkboxes are gone, and the chapter gained substantial new unique content that finally differentiates it from Ch.13. Chapters 6, 9, and 15 came back unchanged from their last-checked state — in particular, **Chapter 9's "Hot Sugarcane Juice" pastime remains the book's single most important open fidelity item**, still unverified against its source after multiple edit passes.

**Third update (Ch.3 re-verified once more):** **Chapter 3 is now fully fixed** — all four remaining items (the unsupported "two-legged animal" phrase, the unsupported maṅgala-ārati detail, the Maya/māyā diacritic, and the softened "This is spiritual laziness!" line) were corrected with careful, source-matched wording rather than just deletions, and the chapter even picked up a bonus restoration of the authentic "eyes" metaphor that was only ever a nice-to-have. This is the best-executed fix pass of any chapter so far.

**Fourth update — Chapter 9's "Hot Sugarcane Juice" pastime is RESOLVED.** The book's longest-standing open fidelity question is closed: the passage is genuine and verified word-for-word in source 12 (the same talk Ch.12 draws from), in the Q&A tail right after the Satyabhāmā exchange. The only real issue was a misattribution — the source explicitly credits this teaching to **Śrī Rādhāgovinda Mahārāj**, not to Gurudeva's own spiritual master Bhakti Tīrtha Swami as the chapter currently states. Sub-headers have since also been added, fully fixing the structure item. Only the one-line attribution fix remains open.

**Fifth update — Chapter 17 is now fully fixed.** All four pastime-fidelity items (the Brahmana/cowherd-boy messenger swap, the unsupported Rūpa Gosvāmī addition, the dropped Śaṅkarācārya accusation/vindication frame, and the two dropped vivid details) have been corrected precisely, restoring the chapter to full alignment with its source. Alongside Ch.3, this is one of the two cleanest, most complete fix passes in the whole book.

**Sixth update (Ch.16, 18, 19 re-checked):** Real progress on Ch.18 (Heart Check hour-counting removed) and Ch.19 (source-slavery wording now matches even more closely, only 1 of 4 items left). Ch.16 barely moved (1 of 4 items lightly improved). **But a new regression appeared in Chapter 19: its Heart Check no longer closes with a personal prayer to Gurudev/Kṛṣṇa like every other chapter in the book — it now ends with a "Signed: ___ Date: ___" commitment format**, which drifts toward exactly the pledge-card/worksheet tone the book's own anti-corporate Heart Check rule warns against, even without a literal checkbox. This wasn't present in the prior version and should be caught before it spreads to other chapters. Ch.18 also picked up a small oddity: a Heart Check item about practicing "tolerance, humility, or freedom from envy" that reads like it was carried over from Ch.17's Kāliya/anartha theme rather than written for Ch.18's death-preparation theme.

**Eighth update — Chapter 5's misattribution is fixed and confirmed.** The diary/GBC-meeting/mystic-power/deathbed-donation stories now correctly read "Bhakti Tīrtha Swami" throughout — the "Bhattacharya"/"Bhakti Cāru Svāmī" confusion (pure ASR noise, confirmed by the author, same failure pattern as Ch.15's "Bhakti Chitra") has been fully reverted. The GBC standoff and "fourfold protection" material are also now appropriately hedged as reported narration rather than overclaimed verbatim quotes. This chapter is effectively resolved — only three low-priority stylistic items remain (bulleted Battlefield list, missing Narottama dāsa in the ācārya list, hybrid salutation).

**Twelfth update — Chapter 18 is now fully resolved, closing out the last open item in the entire book.** "The Test of Death" section — untraceable across four separate check-ins — has been entirely replaced with "Preparing for the Inevitable Exit," and I verified the new content word-for-word against Prabhupāda's actual purport to SB 2.3.1 (the term *manīṣiṇām*, the 8.4-million-species evolutionary cycle, bhakti-yoga as "a revival of the lost relation," all genuinely present in the source). **Every chapter in the book, plus the front matter, is now fully resolved.**

**Eleventh update — Chapter 19 is now fully resolved.** The Heart Check regression (the "Signed: ___ Date: ___" commitment format that had replaced the personal prayer ending) is fixed — it now closes like every other chapter, with a proper prayer to Kṛṣṇa/Gurudev. The Caitanya Mahāprabhu attribution has also been softened to a safe, unattributed "our teachers explain," resolving the last open fidelity item. No open items remain in this chapter.

**Tenth update — Chapter 16 is now fully resolved.** The "2,000 gallons of blood" attribution to Śrīla Bhaktisiddhānta Sarasvatī Ṭhākura was confirmed correct by the author (not an error after all). The Surabhi project line now correctly scopes the present-day revival to Nigeria only, separate from Gurudeva's historical Nigeria/Ghana farm communities. The "died before now" line has been restored to the source's starker original wording. No open items remain in this chapter.

**Ninth update — Chapters 13 & 14 fully restructured, verified against both sources.** Acting on a proposed source-mapping analysis, both chapters were rebuilt around their correct native transcripts (Ch.13 → source 14; Ch.14 → source 13). I independently read both source files in full and confirmed every major claim: Ch.13 now correctly holds the Empty Classroom metaphor, the Orphan Mentality, Prabhupāda's householder story, and the phone call (all from source 14); Ch.14 now correctly holds the actual crying-queen verse with the right translation ("shook her breasts"), the Arjuna/Duryodhana hearing test, the Powerhouse analogy, the Caretaker Sweeper story, and "Maintenance is Maya" (all from source 13). The two chapters no longer overlap at all — this resolves the single longest-standing structural problem in the book and is the best content-level fix of this entire review.

**Seventh update (Ch.18 & 21 re-checked):** Ch.18 unchanged — "The Test of Death" section is still the one open item in this chapter, and appears untouched since the last check. **Ch.21 saw major, well-executed improvements**: two of the four remaining unsourced specifics were fixed (multi-million-dollar temples softened to match source; Prabhupāda's penniless/heart-attack details replaced with vaguer, source-safe language), the Muslim madrasas claim was simplified toward the source's actual brief mention, and — most notably — **the chapter now has a genuine capstone ending**, explicitly bridging back to Chapter 1's "heads on the ground" image in its final paragraph. This resolves the book-ending concern raised in the original audit and is some of the most thoughtful editorial work found in this whole review. Only the wedding-cost rupee figures and the Ramanujacharya diacritics remain open in Ch.21.
