The sinful Pralambasura having been killed, the demigods felt extremely happy and they showered flower garlands upon Lord Balarama and praised the excellence of his deeds. Thus end the purport of the humble servant of the divine grace, Sri Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada to the tenth Canto, eighteenth chapter of the Srimad-Bhagavatam entitled Lord Balarama Slays the Demon Pralambasura.
